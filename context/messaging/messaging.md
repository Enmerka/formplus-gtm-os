Source key: [CO](../docs/research/company.md), [CU](../docs/research/customers.md), [CP](../docs/research/competitors.md), [VO](../docs/research/voice.md).

## Core message

**One form takes the details, the documents and the payment, so nobody has to type up paper or chase what's missing.**

Built from Formplus's own line "Replace paper forms with a robust online form builder and data collection tool." [VO] and what customers bought [CU].

## Message pillars

| Pillar | What it says | Proof points |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Off paper, into one record** | Applications and registrations arrive online and complete, not on paper to type up | Polk State: "We were manually entering students into a spreadsheet once they completed a paper application." Now "100% faster to obtain enrollment applications" and "decreased our time and cost ten times over" [CU]. Performance Equipment: "nearly 10,000 entries via forms" [CU] |
| **2. Files without the sign-in** | Respondents upload documents and photos without a Google account | Google Forms uploads require a Google sign-in that can't be turned off [CP]. Total Sec Oy left Google Forms because attachments required signing in [CU]. Yellow Dandelion chose Formplus because "it allowed file uploads" [CU]. Files go to Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive [CO] |
| **3. Get paid in the same form** | Fees and orders are paid inside the form, with no Formplus fee on top | "We do not charge a fee to integrate a form with any of our payment partners." Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, Flutterwave [CO]. PAB: students "register themselves for our online classes and pay for them" [CU]. Beauty Bar Group "cut the steps involved in their sales funnel by 60%" [CU] |
| **4. Live in a day, free to start** | A small team can set it up alone and start free | "Start for free", "No credit card required" [CO]. PAB: "up and running with Formplus in a matter of just a day or two" [CU]. Career Consultant: "receive CVs the same day which we were able to do" [CU] |

## Angle per persona

| Persona | Lead pillar | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Champion (admissions, registrar, program manager) | 1 and 2 | Stop retyping applications and chasing documents; everything arrives in one record. Proof: Polk State, COTSA, AASHE [CU] |
| Economic buyer (founder, director, COO) | 3 and 4 | Take the booking or order and the payment together, at a fraction of a custom system's cost. Proof: Watamu Treehouse, Beauty Bar Group, Motive [CU] |
| Influencer (IT, data, finance) | 2, plus stated security | Sensitive files arrive by secure form, not email; money goes straight to the organisation. Proof: Total Sec Oy; payments go "directly to the Form Owner or User" [CU] [CO] |

## Words and claims to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Em dashes | CLAUDE.md rule |
| User counts ("thousands", "70K+", "1429+") | Formplus's own figures conflict [CO] |
| "Offline" as a headline | Labelled "(beta)" [VO]; no customer proof [CU] |
| AI | No AI feature found [VO] |
| SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO, NDPA, "enterprise-grade" | Not found [CO] |
| "Seamless", "powerful", "superb" | Formplus repeats these [VO]; every competitor says them too (INFERRED); prefer the customer's result |
| Competitor prices | Third-party and unverified [CP] |
