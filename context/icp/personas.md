# Formplus buyer personas

Built only from `context/docs/research/` (checked 2026-11-13). Quotes are verbatim from customers or reviewers, with the speaker and source. INFERRED = our reading, not a stated fact. Segment letters refer to [icp.md](icp.md).

Source key: [CO](../docs/research/company.md) company, [CU](../docs/research/customers.md) customers, [CP](../docs/research/competitors.md) competitors, [MK](../docs/research/market.md) market, [SG](../docs/research/signals.md) signals, [VO](../docs/research/voice.md) voice.

## Is the economic buyer a separate person?

| Team type | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Small teams (segments D, E, small B) | **Same person.** The founder or director finds, uses and pays | Founders or heads quoted as the hands-on user: Amanda (Yellow Dandelion, owner and two daughters), Paul (Watamu founder, 3 to 45 staff), Brooke Ziegler (B.E Naturale founder), Ochuko (Beauty Bar CEO), Sarah Berry (Career Consultant Director). Team size is stated only for Yellow Dandelion and Watamu [CU] |
| Larger organisations (segments A, F, larger B) | **Often separate.** A staff champion runs the forms; the person who approves spend is not named | Champions here are staff: Instructional Technologist (Polk), Senior Consultant (Kellton, 1001 to 5000 staff), Marketing Analyst (We Insure) [CU]. Formplus has a "How to Convince Your Team (and Boss) About Formplus" page [VO] |

INFERRED: at $20 to $85 a month [CO], a champion in a mid-size organisation can probably buy on a card without a formal process. Not confirmed by any source.

## 1. Champion: the operations or program owner

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Job titles | Instructional Technologist, Operations Coordinator, Senior Education Manager, Membership & Data Manager, Data & Program Support, Marketing Analyst, Client Relationships Director, Senior Consultant (People Practice) [CU]; M&E or MEAL officer [SG] |
| Segments | A, B, F, larger D |
| Measured on (INFERRED from what they praise) | Time spent processing submissions; complete, consistent data; response rates; deadlines met. Inspirock reported "Improved response rate"; We Insure: "We avoid chasing missing information or dealing with inconsistent fields." [CU] |

**Top three pains, in their words**
1. Manual re-keying: "We were manually entering students into a spreadsheet once they completed a paper application." (Virginia Richard, Instructional Technologist, Polk State) [CU]
2. Collecting files and documents: "We needed a way to collect URLS and documents for our early submission events in advance of our conference." (Tony Raymond, State Advisor, COTSA) [CU]
3. Manual follow-up work: "We used to create certificates manually" (Daita Serghi, Senior Education Manager, AASHE) [CU]

**What makes them start looking**
- A deadline or event: admission windows, conferences, annual kickoffs [CU] [MK]
- The current tool hits a wall: Google Forms needs respondents to sign in to upload ("receiving attachments requires signing in...", Total Sec Oy) [CU]
- Team access breaks: SurveyMonkey's teams option "didn't give us the functionality we needed" (NPTC) [CU]

**Objections**
- "Where Formplus may fall short as your needs grow" (Jotform's framing) [CP]
- "I really struggled with using the calculations" (Laura C, reviewer) [CO]
- Team limits: 3 teams and 10 members on Starter [CO]
- Already trained on KoboToolbox or ODK (NGO M&E) [SG]

## 2. Economic buyer: the founder, director or COO

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Job titles | Founder, CEO, Director, COO, Managing Director [CU] |
| Segments | D, E, small B. Also signs off in A and F (title not named in research) |
| Measured on (INFERRED from what they praise) | Orders and revenue, cost, staff time. Yellow Dandelion: "200% increase in orders"; Watamu: "at a fraction of the cost of a custom system"; Beauty Bar "cut the steps involved in their sales funnel by 60%" [CU] |

**Top three pains, in their words**
1. Keeping up with records: "The major challenge was with keeping up with our database of reporting, brand ambassadors, and just keeping things..." (Brooke Ziegler, Founder, B.E Naturale) [CU]
2. Paper slowing the business: "Unlike when we were using pen and paper, we can now save time and focus on other important things" (Ochuko, CEO, Beauty Bar Group) [CU]
3. Taking registration and payment in one step: "We needed somewhere to collect all the athlete information and take payment together" (Rebecca, Motive Movement, title not stated) [CU]

**What makes them start looking**
- Sales outgrow phone, DM and paper (Beauty Bar; Yellow Dandelion at 5 orders a week) [CU]
- An add-on or workaround stops working (Ruby's Place's Google Forms payment add-on was discontinued) [CU]
- A forced move online (PAB studio closed during COVID; Watamu needed "a contactless way to register guests") [CU]

**Objections**
- Price: "I felt that the free features are limited and the paid features are a bit expensive." (Rie T, reviewer) [CO]
- Terms: "Commercial policy, subscription terms" (G2 reviewer, snippet) [CO]
- Free options: Google Forms, Tally (unlimited free), KoboToolbox (free for most users) [CP]
- Pricing is in USD; Naira pricing Not found (open question for segment E) [CO]

## 3. Influencer: IT, data and compliance reviewers, plus the people who use the forms

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Job titles | IT Manager (Performance Equipment) [CU]; M&E officer, data quality manager [SG]; team members who approve or deny applications [CU]; accounting staff (YMCA accounting forms, Perpetual CPA) [CU] |
| Segments | A, B, C, F |
| Measured on (INFERRED) | Data security, reliability, fit with existing systems |

**Top three pains, in their words**
1. Collecting from many people in many places: "In the summer of 2015, I was looking for a program that could record information from multiple people from anywhere" (Noah Kriska, IT Manager, Performance Equipment) [CU]
2. Sensitive files over email: "We send and receive sensitive data like tax documents, copies of ID's etc, - things that you can't send with unsecure email." (Miko Linnavirta, Senior partner, Total Sec Oy) [CU]
3. Access friction for users and outside partners: "without having to jump through additional hoops" (Toni Ripper, NPTC, attribution inferred); "One of the key reasons we selected Formplus was that it was globally accessible" (Mike T., We Insure) [CU]

**What makes them start looking**
- External partners cannot use the internal tool (We Insure ruled out Microsoft Forms) [CU]
- Regulator pressure on how personal data is collected: NDPC notices and audits [MK]
- A new reporting duty, such as Global Fund sub-recipients reporting up [MK]

**Objections**
- No SOC 2, HIPAA or ISO claim found; no DPA or sub-processor list; hosting location not stated [CO]
- No NDPA statement; the privacy policy covers GDPR only [CO]
- Offline marked "(beta)" [CO]
- API availability unclear (one listing says no API; unverified) [CO]
- Existing systems: We Insure's systems "integrate only with Microsoft Office or OneDrive" (paraphrase in the story) [CU]
