## Detail by competitor

### Google Forms
- Headline: "Online forms to get insights quickly" / "Easily create forms and surveys to gather data and gain insights from anywhere." Source: https://workspace.google.com/products/forms/ (redirect from google.com/forms/about)
- For: Google Workspace users and businesses.
- Known gap that Formplus customers cite: file uploads require respondents to sign in to a Google account, and this cannot be turned off. Sources: https://formswrite.com/blog/why-can-t-i-upload-a-file-to-google-forms, https://contentsnare.com/google-forms-file-uploads/ (search snippets). Total Sec Oy and B.E Naturale say the same in their Formplus stories (see [customers.md](customers.md)).
- Native payments: Not found in search; Ruby's Place story says their Google Forms payment add-on "was discontinued" (paraphrase), https://www.formpl.us/blog/post/ruby-place
- Offline: Not found in search results.
- Most frequently named "before" tool in Formplus case studies (5 of 21).

### Jotform
- Headline: "EASIEST ONLINE FORM BUILDER" / "Powerful forms get it done." Source: https://www.jotform.com
- For: teams replacing manual work with forms that have logic, payments, reports and automation (paraphrase).
- User count: about 35 million per one third-party source (search snippet); not confirmed.
- How Jotform describes Formplus (https://www.jotform.com/blog/formplus-alternatives/): "Formplus helps teams move data collection online with offline forms, file uploads, payments"; "Formplus can cover a broad range of data collection tasks, particularly for teams that need offline access"; section heading "Where Formplus may fall short as your needs grow".
- INFERRED: Jotform ranks for "Formplus alternatives" and concedes offline as a Formplus strength.

### Typeform
- Headline: "Your favorite forms. Now with AI automation." / "Combine AI forms and automated workflows to drive revenue growth." Source: https://www.typeform.com
- For: businesses focused on revenue, lead capture and follow-up (paraphrase).
- Pricing raised since Dec 2025: Basic $29 to $39, Plus $59 to $79, Business $99 to $129 (https://www.growform.co/typeform-pricing/, search snippet). Free plan 10 responses/month.
- Compliance: SOC 2 and GDPR per https://www.spotsaas.com/compare/formplus-vs-typeform (third-party).
- INFERRED: Typeform has moved upmarket into marketing and revenue use cases, away from operational data collection.

### SurveyMonkey
- Headline (from a tool summary, not verbatim): "Humans still matter. Listen to them." / "Understand the changing needs of your employees, customers, attendees, and buyers with continuous feedback." Source: https://www.surveymonkey.com
- For: organisations wanting continuous feedback (paraphrase).
- Individual plan about $35/month (https://toolradar.com/compare/surveymonkey-vs-microsoft-forms, checked May 2026 by that site).
- NPTC moved from SurveyMonkey to Formplus over team access (https://www.formpl.us/blog/post/nptc-formplus-teams).

### Microsoft Forms
- Free with a personal account; premium within Microsoft 365 from about $6/user/month (third-party snippet).
- Strength for Microsoft 365 shops: exports to Excel and Power BI (search snippet).
- We Insure Group ruled it out because it "wouldn't be accessible to our external partners" (paraphrase in the story), https://www.formpl.us/blog/post/powering-an-award-winning-insurance-franchise-network-we-insure-groups-approach-to-structured-data
- Product page headline (en-WW and en-IN versions): "Create surveys and quizzes in minutes—get insights that matter." Source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-ww/microsoft-365/online-surveys-polls-quizzes (search snippet, 2026-11-13; en-US wording not confirmed).
- Microsoft Learn: "a simple, lightweight app that lets you easily create surveys, quizzes, and polls"; available in Microsoft 365 Business Basic, Standard, Premium and Apps for enterprise; up to 50,000 responses per form. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-forms/set-up-microsoft-forms (search snippet, 2026-11-13).

### KoboToolbox
- Homepage fetch returned 403. Headline: Not found.
- Offline collection is part of its plans (https://www.kobotoolbox.org/blog/supporting-future-growth-and-accessibility-introducing-kobotoolboxs-new-plan-model, search snippet).
- Free Community tier with about 1,000 submissions/month and 1GB (third-party snapshot May 2026, https://www.xpay.sh/saas-pricing/kobotoolbox/). Another review site says 5,000. Paid tier prices conflict.

Source: https://www.kobotoolbox.org/blog/supporting-future-growth-and-accessibility-introducing-kobotoolboxs-new-plan-model (search snippet, 2026-11-13; post undated in results).
- 2025 highlights post title mentions "1 billion surveys and a decade of community-led impact". Source: https://www.kobotoolbox.org/blog/kobo-2025-highlights-1-billion-surveys-and-a-decade-of-community-led-impact (search result title, 2026-11-13).
- A Nigerian careers guide says KoboToolbox is the most widely used platform across NGOs in Nigeria (https://profolio.ng/monitoring-and-evaluation-officer/how-to-get-a-job, search snippet).
- Nigerian NGO M&E job ads in 2026 ask for "ODK or KoboToolbox" experience (see [signals.md](signals.md)).

## Observations (INFERRED)

- Two competitor groups: general form builders (Jotform, Typeform, Tally, Zoho, Paperform, Fillout, Google, Microsoft), most now leading with "AI"; and field data tools (KoboToolbox, ODK, SurveyCTO, CommCare) leading with offline and data quality.
- Formplus's homepage does not mention AI. It leads with "paperless" and offline.
- Formplus's entry price ($20 to $25) is below Jotform and Typeform entry prices quoted above, and above Tally's free plan.
- Formplus supports Paystack and Flutterwave; none of the competitor pages checked mention African gateways (not checked in depth).
