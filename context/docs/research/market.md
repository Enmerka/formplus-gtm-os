## Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and NDPC enforcement

- GAID 2025 (General Application and Implementation Directive) issued by the NDPC on 20 March 2025; one source says effective 19 September 2025. Primary text: https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf (not read in full). Summaries: https://privacymatters.dlapiper.com/2025/06/nigeria-ndpc-issues-gaid-key-compliance-insights/, https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/privacy-protection/1606106/the-nigeria-data-protection-commission-issues-the-general-application-and-implementation-directive-2025-gaid
- Data controllers and processors "of major importance" (DCPMIs) must register with the NDPC; three tiers: Ultra-High, Extra-High, Ordinary-High. Thresholds and fees in Schedule 7 of the GAID. Must appoint a Data Protection Officer. Sources: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/privacy-protection/1627428/key-regulatory-updates-under-the-2025-general-application-and-implementation-directive-gaid-issued-by-the-nigeria-data-protection-commission-ndpc, https://blog.lawpavilion.com/ndp-act-2023-gaid-2025-a-comprehensive-guide-to-nigerias-new-data-protection-landscape
- Annual Compliance Audit Returns (CAR): normally due 31 March. The 2025 CAR deadline was extended to 30 May 2026. Sources: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1769986/the-ndpc-has-extended-the-deadline-for-filing-2025-data-protection-compliance-audit-returns-to-30-may-2026, https://oal.law/?p=1010450. Next CAR (for 2026) would fall on 31 March 2027 if no change (INFERRED from the standard timeline).
- Penalties: up to the higher of ₦10 million or 2% of annual gross revenue for major controllers; ₦2 million or 2% for others (law-firm summary, not checked against the Act). Source: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1736914/data-protection-compliance-in-nigeria-audit-return-obligations-for-2026
- August 2025: NDPC notice to more than 1,300 organisations to show compliance within 21 days. Source: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1675960/regulatory-update-ndpc-sets-21-day-deadline-for-data-protection-compliance
- Fines issued: MultiChoice Nigeria ₦766,242,500; Fidelity Bank ₦555.8 million. Source: search snippets via https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/privacy-protection/1786536/updates-on-the-recent-activities-and-other-developments-in-the-data-privacy-sector
- Education sector: on 19 February 2026 the NDPC issued a Compliance Notice to 649 tertiary institutions (universities, polytechnics, colleges), 21 days to respond. ITWeb reports "over 1,000 educational institutions". Sources: https://www.tvcnews.tv/ndpc-probes-tertiary-institutions-over-compliance-issues, https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-probes-schools-over-data-privacy-compliance/JBwErvn38R576Db2, https://nigeriannewsdirect.com/ndpc-launches-jationwide-probe-into-universities-over-alleged-data-privacy-breaches/
- August 2026: NDPC forensic probe into UNILAG, Lotus Bank and Hackerbella over alleged unauthorised use of students' data, covering impact assessments, profiling and data sharing. Sources: https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/902331-ndpc-begins-forensic-probe-of-unilag-lotus-bank-hackerbella-over-alleged-unlawful-use-of-students-data.html, https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/12/ndpc-probes-unilag-lotus-bank-over-alleged-unauthorised-student-bank-accounts/
- INFERRED relevance: schools and organisations that collect personal data on paper or through ad-hoc tools now face audit and consent duties. Formplus states GDPR compliance but no NDPA-specific claim was found on formpl.us (Not found).

## GDPR (EU and UK buyers)

- Cumulative GDPR fines about EUR 6.11 billion (CMS Enforcement Tracker 7th edition, 2,685 fines as of March 2026). Source: https://cms.law/en/int/publication/GDPR-Enforcement-Tracker-Report/executive-summary
- Annual fines about EUR 1.2 billion for years ending Jan 2025 and Jan 2026 (secondary synthesis). Source: https://compliancehub.wiki/gdpr-enforcement-and-data-breach-landscape-a-synthesis-of-2025-2026-trends/
- Most common fine reasons: "Insufficient legal basis for data processing" and non-compliance with general processing principles. Source: CMS link above.
- EDPB 2026 Coordinated Enforcement Framework focuses on transparency and information obligations (privacy notices, consent, employee information). Source: CMS link above (announced October 2025).
- INFERRED relevance: forms are where consent and privacy notices are captured. Formplus states "Formplus is also GDPR Compliant" (https://www.formpl.us/pricing) but details on DPA, sub-processors or EU hosting: Not found.

## Donor and grant reporting

- FCDO accountable grants: reports in multi-donor settings must "clearly segregate the FCDO proportion of funding"; partners publish IATI activity files "at least quarterly". Sources: https://www.grtd.fcdo.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/FCDO-Accountable-Grant-Arrangement-Template.docx, https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fcdo-iati-guidelines/fcdo-iati-guidelines (search snippets)
- Global Fund: Principal Recipients submit a Progress Update and Disbursement Request (PU/DR) combining programmatic and financial progress; the PR "aggregates sub-recipient data and submits a single consolidated report". Sources: https://resources.theglobalfund.org/en/grant-life-cycle/grant-implementation/pr-reporting/, https://healthimplementation.undp.org/functional-areas/financial-management/grant-reporting/progress-update-disbursement-request/ (search snippets, 2026-11-13).
- PU/DR is submitted through the Global Fund Partner Portal (January 2025 presentation). Source: https://resources.theglobalfund.org/media/15355/cr_2025-01-progress-update-disbursement-request-partner-portal_presentation_en.pdf
- Frequency (UNDP guidance, verify with Global Fund): High Impact and Core countries also submit Pulse Checks in Q1 and Q3; Focused countries submit an annual PU/DR. Source: UNDP link above.
- INFERRED relevance: sub-recipients (local NGOs) must send structured program data up to the PR on a fixed cycle.
- Gates Foundation grantee reporting requirements: Not found.
- USAID cuts (2025): USAID previously funded household surveys, satellite analysis and program evaluations; field research scaled back. Source: https://www.engineeringforchange.org/news/field-research-has-halted-after-usaid-funding-cuts/
- More than two-thirds of national statistical offices reported funding reductions since January 2025; 73% report negative impacts on SDG monitoring data. Source: https://nextbillion.net/news/analysis-the-data-crisis-following-usaids-withdrawal-opportunities-to-reimagine-data-systems (search snippet)
- IFES survey: 84% of respondents lost funding in 2025 due to foreign aid cuts. Source: https://www.ifes.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/IFES%20Surveying%20the%20Global%20Democracy%20Sector%202025-ForReview.pdf
- UN OCHA (via snippet) identified resource-intensive in-person data collection as most at risk from cuts. Source: https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/opinion/2025/03/10/data-streams-underpin-humanitarian-response-are-about-collapse (linked opinion piece; OCHA original not read)
- INFERRED relevance: two-way pressure. NGOs have less budget (bad for paid tools) but must do more M&E with fewer staff and cheaper tools (good for a low-cost tool with offline and reporting). Not confirmed by any source.

## Move off paper forms

- US Chamber of Commerce: paper-based government forms cost federal agencies $37.8B (Nextgov, Oct 2022); the Chamber's own page cites "$117 billion" cost to Americans; 106 billion forms processed annually. Conflicting figures. Sources: https://www.nextgov.com/it-modernization/2022/10/paper-based-government-forms-cost-federal-agencies-378b/378566/, https://uschamber.com/improving-government/government-digitization-means-less-waste-better-access
- 21% of businesses globally still use paper forms (SOTI, citing its Mobility in Motion report; not verified). Source: https://soti.net/resources/blog/2024/paper-based-processes-cost-ceo-s-more-than-they-know/
- 30% to 50% of schools use a mix of digital and paper (Adobe K-12 infographic, vendor). Source: https://www.adobe.com/content/dam/dx-dc/pdf/ue/k-12-doc-digitization-infographic-ue.pdf
- Manual entry from paper "approximately 20%" error rate (PlatoForms whitepaper, vendor). Source: https://www.platoforms.com/assets/whitepaper/platoforms-global-form-digitization-automation-industry-whitepaper.pdf
- Nigeria-specific paper-forms data: Not found.
- Formplus case studies that replaced paper: Polk State, Beauty Bar Group, Performance Equipment, COTSA, Watamu Treehouse (see [customers.md](customers.md)).
