## Case studies (https://www.formpl.us/case-studies)

- URL: https://www.formpl.us/case-studies/career-consultant
- Industry: recruiting / HR consultancy. Country: United Kingdom. Size: Not stated.

- Results: "Receiving thousands of responses since they started using Formplus".

### 2. Beauty Bar Group
- URL: https://www.formpl.us/case-studies/beauty-bar-group

## Patterns (all INFERRED from the stories above)

- Industries that recur: education (Polk, COTSA, PAB, NPTC, AASHE), non-profits (AASHE, Ruby's Place, YMCA, MMPA, Pact, ADRA), small e-commerce (Beauty Bar, Yellow Dandelion), events and registrations (We Insure, I Made It, Motive), professional services (Perpetual CPA, Career Consultant).
- Most named customers are small teams; the only stated large one is Kellton Tech (1001-5000).
- What they replaced: paper (Polk, Beauty Bar, Performance Equipment, COTSA, Watamu), Google Forms (Career Consultant, Total Sec, Polk, COTSA, Ruby's Place), email (Kellton, Inspirock, MMPA, AASHE), SurveyMonkey (NPTC), Wufoo (COTSA).
- Recurring reasons: file uploads without Google sign-in, payments in the form, team access, price.
- Nigeria: one story (Beauty Bar Group). Africa total: two (plus Watamu, Kenya). No Nigerian NGO, school or government story found.
- No story mentions offline field collection, despite offline being a headline feature.
