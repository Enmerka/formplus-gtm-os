Raw research, not conclusions. Checked 2026-11-13. Each signal is linked to evidence from Formplus's own customers (what triggered their purchase) or public sources. Anything not shown directly by a source is marked INFERRED.

## 3. Regulatory and calendar signals

- Named in the NDPC compliance notice to 649 tertiary institutions (19 Feb 2026); newspapers published the list that day. Source: https://www.tvcnews.tv/ndpc-probes-tertiary-institutions-over-compliance-issues. INFERRED: the published list is a usable target list of Nigerian institutions with data-handling pressure.
- Organisations among the 1,300+ that got the August 2025 NDPC 21-day notice. Source: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1675960/regulatory-update-ndpc-sets-21-day-deadline-for-data-protection-compliance. Whether the list is public: Not found.
- DCPMI register: the NDPC is said to publish and update the list of registered DCPMIs yearly (secondary source: https://blog.lawpavilion.com/ndp-act-2023-gaid-2025-a-comprehensive-guide-to-nigerias-new-data-protection-landscape).
- Compliance Audit Returns normally due 31 March each year (2025 return extended to 30 May 2026). See [market.md](market.md).
- Nigerian university post-UTME screening forms about July to September; admissions lists September to November. See [market.md](market.md).
- Nigerian secondary and pre-degree schools announcing form sales (e.g. LASU International School, LASU Staff School, UNILAG Foundation Studies for 2026/2027), often with payment by bank transfer. See [market.md](market.md). INFERRED: a school announcing form sales with a bank-transfer and paper process is a timely target.
- Global Fund sub-recipients reporting into a Principal Recipient on a fixed PU/DR cycle. See [market.md](market.md).
- INFERRED: annual events (conferences, kickoffs, competitions, camps) recur on a calendar; the time to reach organisers is before registration opens.

## 4. Website signals (INFERRED; not tested at scale)

- Downloadable PDF or Word forms on a site ("download the application form", "print and submit").
- Embedded Google Forms (docs.google.com/forms links) on admissions, registration or vendor pages, especially with file uploads or payments requested elsewhere.
- "Send your CV / documents to [email]" instructions (Career Consultant and Kellton pattern).
- Orders taken via WhatsApp or Instagram DM with no order form (Beauty Bar pattern).
- Payments by bank transfer with "send proof of payment" instructions. Common in Nigeria (INFERRED, not sourced).
