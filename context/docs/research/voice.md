## Patterns in the voice (INFERRED)

- Words they repeat: "seamless", "powerful", "simple", "paperless", "no code", "data collection".
- Openers are often questions to the reader ("Are you a small business owner...", "Do you need to receive data from field agents...").
- Positioning is functional and broad ("for everyone", "small and large teams"), not tied to one industry or role.
- No use of "AI" found on any page read, unlike most competitors (see [competitors.md](competitors.md)).
- Spelling mixes UK and US ("analyse", "optimize", "Organizing").
- Minor errors on site: "PCI Complaint", "Data Encyption", "respondent's" (possessive used for plural).
- Historic description from a third party, not Formplus: "a Nigerian SaaS startup that helps businesses gather and process data from their online and offline customers" (https://www.startuplist.africa/startups/formplus, search snippet).
