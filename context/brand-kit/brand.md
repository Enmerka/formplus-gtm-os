## Tone of voice (INFERRED from the pages read)

| Trait | Evidence in their copy |
|---|---|
| Plain and functional | "Collect The Information You Need"; "Let forms do the work" |
| Speaks to the reader with a question | "Are you a small business owner looking to receive payments online?"; "Do you need to receive data from field agents in real-time?" |
| Broad, not tied to one industry | "Powerful form building solution for everyone"; "Superb data collection for small and large teams" |
| Promises speed and no code | "Create your first responsive survey in minutes with no code."; "All quizzes can be made in less than 5 minutes with no coding required at all." |
| Leads with paperless | "Switch to a paperless office with functional online and offline (beta) forms" |

## House rules

- No em dashes in any copy (CLAUDE.md).
- Spelling on their site mixes UK and US ("analyse", "optimize"). Pick one per market (INFERRED); US for A-US campaigns.
- Words they repeat: "seamless", "powerful", "simple", "paperless", "no code", "data collection". Use the plain ones; let customer results carry the claims.
- Don't copy their site errors: "PCI Complaint", "Data Encyption", "respondent's" for the plural.
- "Offline" always carries its "(beta)" label in their copy. Keep it.
- No AI language: none in their copy.
