# Competitive positioning

Every claim traces to `context/docs/research/` (checked 2026-11-13). Competitor prices are third-party figures in [competitors.md](../docs/research/competitors.md) and unverified; check vendor pricing pages before quoting them.

Source key: [CO](../docs/research/company.md), [CU](../docs/research/customers.md), [CP](../docs/research/competitors.md), [VO](../docs/research/voice.md), [SG](../docs/research/signals.md).

Formplus for comparison: free plan 50 submissions/mo and 3 forms; Starter $25/mo ($20 yearly) [CO].

## Where Formplus wins

| Win | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Against Google Forms when files or payments are needed | Google Forms uploads require a Google sign-in that can't be turned off [CP]. Total Sec Oy and Ruby's Place switched for this [CU] |
| Against paper | Five stories replaced paper, with results: Polk State "ten times over", Performance Equipment "nearly 10,000 entries via forms" [CU] |
| Price at entry | Starter at $25/mo ($20 yearly) sits below the Jotform and Typeform entry prices above [CO] [CP] |
| African payments | Paystack and Flutterwave beside Stripe and PayPal; "perfect for Africans who want to receive payments from anyone, anywhere in the world" [CO] [VO] |
| Support | Reviewers praise it: "The customer service is great even though it is through a chat." [CO] |
| Fast start | PAB: "up and running with Formplus in a matter of just a day or two" [CU] |

## Where Formplus loses

| Loss | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Review base and social proof** | About 12 to 13 G2 reviews [CO]. G2's own summary: users "feel more confident in the reliability and performance of Formstack compared to Formplus" because Formstack has more reviews [CO]. Jotform claims about 35 million users (third-party, unverified) [CP]. Jotform's review count is not in research. The SpotSaaS reviews shown date from 2022 to 2023 [CO] |
| **Scale claims are inconsistent** | Homepage shows "1429+" trusted users; another page says "Thousands of organizations"; 2018 coverage said "70K+ users" [CO] |
| **Free plan** | 50 submissions and 3 forms, against Tally's unlimited free plan [CO] [CP]. A reviewer: "the free features are limited and the paid features are a bit expensive." [CO] |
| **Design** | "Design and personalization are not good enought." (reviewer) [CO]. Typeform leads on design [CP] |
| **Offline field data** | Offline labelled "(beta)" [VO]; KoboToolbox is free for most users and named in NGO job ads [CP] [SG] |
| **Compliance** | No SOC 2, HIPAA or ISO claim found; Typeform lists SOC 2 (third-party) [CO] [CP] |
| **Already inside Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace** | Microsoft Forms is included in Microsoft 365 business plans [CP]; Google Forms is free |
| **AI** | Jotform, Typeform, Zoho and Fillout lead with AI; no AI claim found on Formplus pages [CP] [VO] |

## How to respond

| When the buyer says | Respond with |
|---|---|
| "We already use Google Forms" | Ask whether families or applicants must sign in to upload files. If yes, that's the switch reason (Total Sec Oy, COTSA) [CU] [CP] |
| "Jotform is bigger" | Don't argue scale. Lead with a same-segment story with numbers (Polk State, Beauty Bar Group) [CU] and the lower entry price [CO] |
| "Tally is free" | Payments and file storage routing matter more than form count for these buyers: Formplus takes Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe and PayPal with no Formplus fee [CO] |
| "We use KoboToolbox" | Don't compete on offline field data. Move on unless they also need payments or registrations |
| "Is it SOC 2 / HIPAA / NDPA compliant?" | State only what's published: SSL, AES-256 at rest, GDPR, PCI processors [CO]. Pass anything else to Formplus; don't guess |
| "It looks plain" | Concede. White-labelled forms are on Professional and above [CO] |
