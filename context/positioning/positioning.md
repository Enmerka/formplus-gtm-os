## Positioning statement

For schools, nonprofits and small service businesses that still collect applications, registrations and orders on paper, PDF or Google Forms, Formplus is an online form builder that takes the details, the documents and the payment in one form, without asking respondents to sign in to Google. Unlike paper or a Google Form with add-ons, files land in the team's own storage and fees arrive through Stripe, PayPal, Paystack or Flutterwave with no Formplus fee on top. Polk State College Charter Schools now gets enrollment applications "100% faster", and Beauty Bar Group "cut the steps involved in their sales funnel by 60%".

## What this positioning does not claim

- AI features: none found on any Formplus page read [VO].
- Offline as the lead: labelled "(beta)" [VO], and no customer story shows offline field collection [CU].
- Compliance beyond GDPR and PCI: SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO and NDPA claims not found [CO].
- Enterprise fit: Platinum caps at 20 members; Enterprise terms are "Contact Us" [CO].
