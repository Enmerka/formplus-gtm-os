# Value proposition by persona

Every claim traces to `context/docs/research/` (checked 2026-11-13). The three personas are the ones defined in [context/icp/personas.md](../icp/personas.md). INFERRED = our reading, not stated in research.

Source key: [CO](../docs/research/company.md), [CU](../docs/research/customers.md), [CP](../docs/research/competitors.md), [VO](../docs/research/voice.md).

## Champion: the operations or program owner

Admissions officer, registrar, program or membership manager, operations coordinator.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Collect applications, registrations or reports, with the files and fees that come with them, in one place the team can work from [CU] |
| **Pain** | "We were manually entering students into a spreadsheet once they completed a paper application." (Virginia Richard, Polk State) [CU]. "We used to create certificates manually" (Daita Serghi, AASHE) [CU]. Google Forms needs a Google sign-in to upload files: "receiving attachments requires signing in" (Total Sec Oy) [CU] [CP] |
| **Gain** | "100% faster to obtain enrollment applications" (Polk State) [CU]. "The Formplus tool has made collection of URLs and forms WAY easier! And it allows us to be all digital." (Tony Raymond, COTSA) [CU]. Team access without hassle: "without having to jump through additional hoops" (NPTC) [CU] |
| **Proof** | Polk State, COTSA, AASHE, NPTC [CU]. Features: file uploads to Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive; team members on every plan (4 on Free up to 20 on Platinum); Form2Doc for documents from submissions [CO] |

## Economic buyer: the founder, director or COO

In small teams this is the same person as the champion (personas.md).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Take orders, bookings or registrations, and the money, without adding staff or a custom system [CU] |
| **Pain** | "Unlike when we were using pen and paper, we can now save time and focus on other important things" (Ochuko, Beauty Bar Group, describing the before state) [CU]. "The major challenge was with keeping up with our database of reporting, brand ambassadors, and just keeping things..." (Brooke Ziegler, B.E Naturale) [CU]. "We needed somewhere to collect all the athlete information and take payment together" (Rebecca, Motive) [CU] |
| **Gain** | "decreased our time and cost ten times over" (Polk State) [CU]. "cut the steps involved in their sales funnel by 60%" (Beauty Bar Group) [CU]. "Formplus already covers about 80% of what we need, at a fraction of the cost of a custom system." (Paul, Watamu Treehouse) [CU] |
| **Proof** | Beauty Bar Group, Watamu Treehouse, Yellow Dandelion, Motive, Ruby's Place [CU]. Price: free plan, paid from $20 to $25 a month; 10% off for nonprofits and schools; "We do not charge a fee to integrate a form with any of our payment partners." [CO] |

## Influencer: IT, data, finance and the people who use the forms

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Make sure data is collected safely, from anyone, and lands where the team already works [CU] |
| **Pain** | "We send and receive sensitive data like tax documents, copies of ID's etc, - things that you can't send with unsecure email." (Miko Linnavirta, Total Sec Oy) [CU]. "I was looking for a program that could record information from multiple people from anywhere" (Noah Kriska, Performance Equipment) [CU] |
| **Gain** | Forms anyone can reach: "One of the key reasons we selected Formplus was that it was globally accessible" (Mike T., We Insure) [CU]. Payments go "directly to the Form Owner or User, and not FormPlus" [CO] |
| **Proof** | Total Sec Oy, Performance Equipment, We Insure Group [CU]. Security stated: 256-bit SSL, data "encrypted at rest using AES-256", "Formplus is also GDPR Compliant", payment processors "PCI compliant" [CO]. Integrations: Google Sheets, Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Zapier, webhooks [CO] |
| **Limits to state honestly** | No SOC 2, HIPAA or ISO claim found; no NDPA statement; data hosting location not found [CO] |
